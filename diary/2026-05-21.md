## What was done
- The RAG-based AI Companion chat is now live at http://ilmai.arzucoder.uz/! The user's query is embedded, and the system performs semantic search to find the closest chunks.
- Implemented strict citation logic. The AI now returns responses with exact source citations like [1] and [2].
- Added typing animations and full Markdown rendering support to the Flutter chat UI.

## Problems encountered
- The Cosine Distance threshold for the vector search was too strict (0.4). Because of this, the AI kept saying "I couldn't find the answer" even when the relevant text was in the document.

## Solutions
- Increased the Cosine Distance threshold to `0.6` (>= 40% similarity). The retrieval accuracy improved significantly without bringing in garbage data.
